Privacy Policy

Rehearsly Speech Coach

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how the iPhone app Rehearsly Speech Coach handles information. On the device the app is named Rehearsly. Its bundle identifier is app.rehearsly.hardfiller. It is offered on the United States App Store, in English, for iPhone.

The developer is Zhiqiang Deng. The Apple Developer team is shown as ZHIQIANG DENG, team ID S8JD26J75G.

What the app does

You record yourself speaking a practice prompt. The app transcribes that recording on the iPhone and shows hard-filler marks, pace, and long pauses. Prompts are included with the app. There is no account and no login.

Information that stays on your iPhone

Recordings, transcripts, filler counts, history, and prompts stay on the device. The app does not upload your audio.

The app stores the following on the iPhone:

  • The recording, as an audio file in the app’s local storage.
  • The transcript, as timed words from that recording.
  • The practice result: the prompt, the scene, the date, the duration, hard-filler marks and their count, words per minute, the long-pause count, a short practice suggestion, and the audio file name.
  • Practice history, in a local file on the device.
  • A practice-quota record in the iPhone Keychain: whether a first session has been completed, the calendar week, and how many sessions were started that week. This record does not contain audio or a transcript.
  • Settings on the device: the practice scene you chose, whether you have seen the welcome screen, and whether local reminders are turned on.

The developer does not receive this information.

Microphone and on-device transcription

The app asks for microphone access so it can record your practice. The permission text states that the recording is not uploaded.

The app asks for speech-recognition access so it can transcribe the recording on the iPhone and mark hard fillers. Transcription uses Apple’s on-device SpeechTranscriber for English (United States). The app reads the audio file on the device. It does not send that audio to the developer, and it does not upload it to a cloud service operated by the developer.

Hard-filler marks come from that on-device transcript. They are not a measured accuracy result. A 20–30 clip accuracy study has not been run, and the app says so on the result screen. This policy does not claim that recognition accuracy is proven.

Speech model download

The first time Apple’s on-device speech model is needed, the app downloads and installs that model. The first speech-model download needs a network connection. If the model is not already on the iPhone, a later session can need a network connection to download it again. That download is the speech model. It is not an upload of your recording.

Apple handles the model download. Apple’s privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. The app does not receive your card number. The developer does not run a separate checkout.

app.rehearsly.pro.monthly $9.99 per month Auto-renewable subscription. No free trial.
app.rehearsly.pro.annual $39.99 per year Auto-renewable subscription with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Apple decides whether an Apple ID is eligible for that introductory trial.

Prices are in US dollars. The app asks Apple for these products and for the purchases already associated with the Apple ID, so it can unlock Pro on the device. You can restore purchases in the app. Choosing manage subscription opens Apple’s subscription page. Billing, renewal, cancellation, and refunds are handled by Apple.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app can schedule local reminders on the iPhone: a daily practice reminder, and a reminder about one day before an annual trial ends. These notifications are created on the device. The app does not register for remote push and does not send a notification token to the developer. You can turn each reminder off in Settings.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

The app does not use analytics or advertising SDKs. It does not include third-party tracking packages. The developer does not sell your information and does not share recordings, transcripts, filler counts, or practice history with third parties.

The app’s network use is limited to the Apple speech-model download described above, Apple In-App Purchase, and opening Apple’s subscription page when you choose to manage a subscription.

Deleting information

In Settings, Delete all recordings removes the recordings and the saved practice results in the app’s local folder on that iPhone. That control does not, by itself, clear the Keychain practice-quota record or the on/off reminder settings.

Removing the app from the iPhone removes the app and the information in its local container. Purchases stay with the Apple ID and can be restored if you install the app again. There is no developer account to delete, because the app does not create one. The developer has no server copy of your recordings or transcripts to erase.
